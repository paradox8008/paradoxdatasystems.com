Privacy Policy

Applies to BookedUp, BookedUp Online and other apps by Paradox Data Systems LLC. Last updated November 7, 2026.

The BookedUp app keeps your information on your own device. If you choose to use BookedUp Online (our optional paid service), a copy of your business information is also stored on our servers so it can sync between your devices, power your online booking page, and send your appointment emails and texts. We never sell your information or your clients’ information, and we don’t show ads.

The BookedUp app, on your device

  • Your business details, services, prices, hours, locations, appointments, client names, phone numbers and emails, notes, sales, expenses, mileage and inventory are saved on your device.
  • Photos you pick or take (receipts, product photos, your logo, payment QR codes) are copied into the app’s own storage on your device.
  • If you delete the app, this information is deleted from the device with it, except backup files you saved elsewhere yourself and anything stored by BookedUp Online.

Backups

BookedUp can save a backup file of your data once a day. The file is kept in the app’s own folder on your device, which your phone’s own backup (iCloud or Google) may include. On Android you can also choose a folder, such as Google Drive, and you can save a copy anywhere you like with the share sheet. Those copies are stored by the service you choose, under that service’s privacy policy. We never receive them.

BookedUp Online

If you sign up for BookedUp Online, we store:

  • Your account: your sign-in email, business name, booking page address and time zone, and the status of your subscription.
  • Your synced business information: the same information the app keeps on your device (services, appointments, clients and their contact details, notes, sales, expenses, inventory and settings), so every device you sign in on and your web dashboard show the same thing.
  • Messages we send for you: a record of the emails and texts sent for your business (who, when, and whether they were delivered), so you can see what went out and we can fix problems.

We use this information only to provide BookedUp to you. It is stored on servers in the United States that we operate or rent from hosting providers. Only you, the people you sign in with, and Paradox Data Systems (to keep the service running and to answer your support requests) can see it.

Companies that help us run the service, and only for that purpose:

  • Amazon Web Services sends appointment emails (and may host our servers).
  • Telnyx sends and receives texts from the shared BookedUp number, for businesses with the texting add-on.
  • Expo delivers notifications to the business owner’s phone.
  • Cloudflare carries and protects traffic to our website and booking pages.
  • Apple, Google and RevenueCat handle subscription purchases made in the app. We receive the subscription status, never your card details.

When your BookedUp Online subscription ends, the information stored on our servers is deleted about 30 days later. The information on your device stays there.

People who book with a BookedUp business

When you book on a business’s BookedUp booking page, you give your name, mobile number, email and any notes you choose to add, and you can choose to get text messages. This information goes to the business you booked with, so they can manage your appointment, and is stored by BookedUp Online for that business. We use it only to confirm, remind you about, change or cancel your appointments with that business, and to send you the emails and texts you asked for. To change or remove your information, contact the business directly, or email us and we’ll pass your request on.

Text messages

  • From the business owner’s own phone. When the owner sends a confirmation, reminder or payment text, the app opens their phone’s Messages app with the text ready, or sends it automatically from their own phone and number (an iPhone Shortcut, or our BookedUp Texting helper app on Android). These texts go through the owner’s own phone carrier.
  • From the BookedUp number (for businesses with the texting add-on). Appointment texts are sent from one shared BookedUp toll-free number through Telnyx, and each text starts with the business’s name. Texts are only sent to people who agreed to get them, either by ticking the box on the booking page or by telling the business they agree. Message frequency varies, usually 2 to 3 texts per appointment. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop all texts from the BookedUp number, START to get them again, and HELP for help. We keep the list of numbers that replied STOP so we can keep honoring it.

We do not sell, rent or share mobile phone numbers or text message opt-in information with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Opt-in information and consent are never shared with anyone, except the companies listed above that deliver the messages. See the texting section of our Terms for the full texting terms.

Email

BookedUp Online sends appointment confirmations, reminders and links to change or cancel by email, for the business you booked with. These are not marketing emails. Each email says which business it is from, and you can reply to reach that business.

Cookies

Our booking and account pages use a few small cookies that are needed for them to work: keeping you signed in, remembering a booking form while you fill it in, and protecting forms from abuse. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell or rent anyone’s information, we don’t show ads, and we don’t use analytics or tracking tools in the app.

Payments

BookedUp never handles card numbers or money for your clients’ visits. Payment buttons and QR codes open apps like Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, Square or Zelle, which have their own privacy policies. BookedUp only records how you were paid.

Permissions

  • Photos, to pick a receipt, product photo, logo or payment QR code.
  • Camera, to take a photo of a receipt or a product, or to scan a product's barcode. Photos you take are kept on your device with that receipt or product. When scanning, the camera only reads the barcode, and nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
  • Contacts, only if you choose to import people from your contacts. The names, numbers and emails you pick are copied into the app on your device.
  • Notifications, to remind you about appointments and tasks.
  • Files, only when you choose a backup folder or a backup file to restore.
  • Text messages (BookedUp Texting helper, Android only), to send appointment texts from your own phone. The helper only sends the texts BookedUp gives it. It does not read your texts.

You can turn any of these off in your phone’s settings.

Security

Information sent to and from BookedUp Online travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to our servers is limited to Paradox Data Systems.

Children

Our apps are business tools and aren’t directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top and, for important changes, let BookedUp Online customers know in the app or by email.
