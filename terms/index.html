Appointment text messages (SMS)

These terms cover BookedUp appointment texts: the texts sent from the BookedUp number for businesses that use BookedUp.

  • What you’ll get: texts about your appointments with the business you booked with: a confirmation when you book, reminders before your appointment, and a notice if it is moved or cancelled. Some businesses also send a thank-you with a link to leave a review. Each text starts with the business’s name. We never send marketing texts.
  • How you sign up: by ticking “Text me a confirmation and appointment reminders” on a business’s BookedUp booking page, or by telling the business you agree to appointment texts. Agreeing to texts is never required to book.
  • How often: message frequency varies with your appointments, usually 2 to 3 texts per appointment.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.
  • To stop: reply STOP to any text. You’ll get one text confirming that you won’t get any more texts from the BookedUp number. Reply START to get them again.
  • For help: reply HELP, contact the business you booked with, or email support@paradoxdatasystems.com.
  • Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Your mobile number and text opt-in are never sold or shared for marketing. See our Privacy Policy.
