BookedUp

Your appointment book, in your pocket.

For one-person businesses that book time: hair, nails, lashes, barbers, tattoo, massage, trainers, groomers, cleaners, tutors.

What it does

Book in seconds

A day timeline in 15-minute rows: tap a time, drag to move, pull to change the length. Double-booking protection you can turn off.

Texts from your own number

Confirmations, reminders and cancellations filled in for you, with the day, time and location. You check it and tap Send.

Checkout your way

Show your Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, Zelle or Square pay code. Tips, retail sales and past-visit totals included.

Money and taxes

Income, expenses with receipt photos, mileage, a tax set-aside and quarterly reminders, plus year-end exports.

Inventory

Track products, what you used on each visit, low-stock alerts and a ready-made resupply list.

Made for your business

Eight looks to start from, your own colors, fonts, icon and words: clients or customers, services or sessions.

Private by design

  • Everything is stored on your phone. No sign-up, no ads, no tracking.
  • Daily backup files you can keep in Google Drive or iCloud, and restore on a new phone.
  • Card numbers never touch the app. Your payment apps handle the money.
