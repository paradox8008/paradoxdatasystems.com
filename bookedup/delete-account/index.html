BookedUp by Paradox Data Systems

Delete your BookedUp Online account

You can delete your BookedUp Online account, and everything we keep for it, at any time. It takes about a minute.

Delete it yourself

  1. In the BookedUp app, open More › BookedUp Online and tap Delete my BookedUp Online account at the bottom. On a computer, go to app.paradoxdatasystems.com/delete-account and sign in with your email.
  2. Type DELETE and tap the red button.

Delete my account

Or ask us

Can’t sign in? Email support@paradoxdatasystems.com from your account’s email address and ask us to delete it. We’ll do it within 7 days and tell you when it’s done.

What’s deleted

  • Your sign-in, and every phone and computer signed in to it.
  • Your business’s synced records: clients, appointments, services, money, stock and settings.
  • Your booking page, pay and manage links, waiting texts and questions.

It happens right away. Our nightly backups roll off within 14 days. If you sent us feedback, we keep the message itself but remove your email address from it. We keep a list of phone numbers that texted STOP so they’re never texted again, as the law requires.

What stays

  • Records on your own phone stay there, and BookedUp keeps working without BookedUp Online. To remove them, use More › Your data › Erase everything on this phone, or uninstall the app.
  • A Google Play or App Store subscription isn’t cancelled by deleting your account. Cancel it in Google Play or in your Apple subscriptions.

Using BookedUp without BookedUp Online? There’s no account to delete. Everything is stored only on your phone. See our privacy policy.
