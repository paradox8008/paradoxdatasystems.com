Automatic texts on an iPhone

iPhones don’t let apps send texts by themselves. Our free Shortcut, BookedUp Texts, picks up your waiting confirmations and reminders at set times and sends them from your own number. You set it up once, in about 5 minutes.

You’ll need BookedUp Online and the Shortcuts app, which comes with every iPhone. If you deleted it, get it free from the App Store.

  1. Copy your texting address

    In BookedUp, open More › BookedUp Online › Automatic texts and choose iPhone. Tap Get my address, then Copy address.

    Your address is private: anyone who has it could read the texts waiting to go out. If you think someone else has it, tap Get a new address and paste the new one into the Text step at the top of the Shortcut.

  2. Add the BookedUp Texts Shortcut

    Add BookedUp Texts

    Open this on your iPhone. Tap Set Up Shortcut, paste your address when it asks for it, and tap Add Shortcut. Keep the name BookedUp Texts: the app’s Send waiting texts now button looks for it by name.

  3. Run it once by hand

    In the Shortcuts app, tap BookedUp Texts once. When your iPhone asks to connect to app.paradoxdatasystems.com, and later to send messages, choose Always Allow.

  4. Make it run by itself

    In Shortcuts, tap Automation, then +. Choose Time of Day, set the time, choose Daily and Run Immediately, and turn Notify When Run off. Tap Next and pick BookedUp Texts.

    Make one automation for each time you want texts to go out. These work well, and you can change them:

    8:00 AM · 10:00 AM · 12:00 PM · 2:00 PM · 4:00 PM · 6:00 PM · 7:30 PM

    Texts go out at these times, not the minute they’re made. An online booking at 8:30 PM gets its confirmation at 8:00 the next morning. Need one out right away? Tap Send waiting texts now in BookedUp.

  5. Send a test text

    Back in Automatic texts, send the test text to your own number, then tap Send waiting texts now. It should arrive in a few seconds, and BookedUp shows it as sent.

If texts stop going out

Nothing has gone out for a while

Open Shortcuts › Automation and check that each automation still says Run Immediately. BookedUp also warns you in Automatic texts when the Shortcut hasn’t checked in for over a day.

The Shortcut asks before sending

Run BookedUp Texts once by hand and choose Always Allow when it asks to connect and to send messages.

I got a new phone

Add the Shortcut again with the button above, paste your address, and make the automations again. Apple doesn’t move automations to a new phone.

On Android? BookedUp Online sends texts with the BookedUp Texting helper instead, and the app walks you through it. Still stuck? Email support@paradoxdatasystems.com.
